    Why AURA matters
    Most health tools react after a number crosses a line. AURA looks at the pattern before that.
    

    🎯The problemSlow changes in sleep, activity and heart rate are easy to miss, so risk often goes unnoticed until it is advanced.
    

    👥Who it helpsDesk-job professionals, families with a history of heart disease or diabetes, and anyone without regular check-ups.
    

    🛣️RoadmapWearable sync, a clinician dashboard, more languages, and validation on real clinical data.
    

    AURA is a prototype for health-risk awareness and decision support. It does not diagnose medical conditions or replace professional medical advice. Sample profiles are synthetic; your own entries are stored in this site’s database.
